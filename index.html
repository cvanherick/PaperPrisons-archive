How to use the archive

Choose your workspace and enter your username and password to unlock it.

Search a word or phrase, or narrow the results by channel, person, and date. Selecting a channel opens its conversations in time order.

Choose View conversation to see the original message and its saved replies together. All times are shown in Pacific time.

Use Lock archive when you finish, especially on a shared computer. The archive also locks after 15 minutes without activity. Reload the page for newly published messages.

For access or password changes, contact your team administrator.
